## 🔍 Understanding Your Numbers

### Savings Rate Calculation
```
Formula: (Income - Expense) ÷ Income × 100
Example: (₹1,50,000 - ₹87,500) ÷ ₹1,50,000 × 100 = 41.67%

Targets:
- 10% = Surviving
- 20% = Comfortable
- 30%+ = Excellent ✨
- 50%+ = Outstanding 🌟
```

### Average Transaction
```
Formula: Total Expense ÷ Number of Transactions
Example: ₹87,500 ÷ 30 transactions = ₹2,917

Lower = More frequent small purchases
Higher = Fewer large purchases
```
