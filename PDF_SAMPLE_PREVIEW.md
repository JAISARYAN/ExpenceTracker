## 🎯 Key Takeaways

1. **First Glance**: Summary cards tell the story instantly
2. **Quick Analysis**: Categories show where money goes
3. **Complete Details**: Every transaction is visible
4. **Smart Insights**: Statistics show financial health
5. **Professional**: Ready to share or print

## 💾 File Information

**Format**: PDF (Portable Document Format)
**Size**: Typically 50-300 KB depending on transaction count
**Pages**: 1-3 typically (auto-expands as needed)
**Colors**: Full RGB color support
**Print**: A4 paper recommended
**Quality**: 72 DPI screen, scalable
